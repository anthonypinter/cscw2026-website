PROGRAM OVERVIEW

This is a preliminary program for CSCW 2026 in-person main conference. We have very limited ability to move presentations and sessions around due to the many scheduling constraints. However, email CSCW 2026 General Chairs Xinru Page and Guo Freeman (chairs2026@cscw.acm.org) by Friday Sept. 4 if you are scheduled for multiple presentations at the same time so we can look into alternatives.
The final program will be available on the SIGCHI web app at a later date. CSCW 2026 will also host an online presentation day on October 1–2, 2026 (depending on the time zone). The online program will be shared later.
