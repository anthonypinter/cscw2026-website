PARENT & FAMILY RESOURCES

CSCW 2026 welcomes attendees traveling with children and families. We have prepared a Parent & Family Welcome Packet with information to help families plan for and navigate the conference.
The packet includes information about on-site family facilities, including the lactation/pumping room and children’s play area and family lounge, as well as nearby kid-friendly attractions, parks, pharmacies, and medical resources in Salt Lake City.
