CALL FOR PAPERS FOR CSCW 2027 AND BEYOND

To present a paper at the ACM CSCW conference, you must have a paper accepted at one of two journals:
  • PACM HCI/CSCW
  • ToCHI (if the paper meets the CSCW Scope criteria on the link above)
Accepted authors will receive email with instructions on how to request a presentation slot. Presenting is encouraged but not required.
There are no submission deadlines for any year’s conference, since papers may go through different number of rounds of review. If you receive an acceptance after the program for this year is set, then you will be offered a presentation slot at the following year’s conference. If you receive an acceptance before the program is set, you may alternately request a slot at the following year’s conference if you prefer.
